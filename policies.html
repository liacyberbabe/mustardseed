Shipping Policy

Shipping & fulfillment

Orders are processed according to inventory availability, order size and destination. Shipping timelines and carrier options are communicated at checkout or during wholesale order confirmation. International customers are responsible for applicable duties, taxes and customs charges unless otherwise stated.

Returns

Returns & exchanges

Wholesale returns must be requested within the period specified on the applicable order confirmation. Products must be unused, unwashed and in original condition. Customized, decorated or otherwise altered goods may not be eligible for return.

Damaged orders

Issues with an order

Please inspect shipments promptly. If merchandise arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, contact Mustard Seed with the order number and clear photographs so the issue can be reviewed.
